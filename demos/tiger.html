Tiger

These Nile pipelines draw the Ghostscript Tiger's 240 SVG paths. Plain TypeScript lexes each path's d attribute into PathCommands, and PathCubics interprets them into cubic curves. SvgFill transforms a path's cubics, flattens them to quadratic beziers, clips them and rasterizes them into coverage spans; SvgStroke transforms and flattens them, then outlines them with gezira's stroker. PaintSpans turns the spans into coloured pixels, which TypeScript writes to the canvas. The pipelines are parameterized by the view matrix (zoom and pan), the SVG's own transform, the clip rectangle and, for strokes, the width, a minimum width, the join and the cap. On the GPU, rasterizing and compositing run as WGSL.
