Tiger

The Ghostscript Tiger: 240 SVG paths, interpreted, filled and stroked by Nile.

See the Nile pipeline 





  The host lexes the SVG into path commands, chooses the view, and writes the
  painted pixels. gezira's kernels, its stroker included, are used unmodified.
  On the GPU, each path's beziers are rasterized and composited in WGSL.



How to use: double-click to zoom in (100%, 200%, 400%,
  then back), and drag to pan. The renderer menu switches between the CPU and
  the GPU.
