Paint

Gezira's textures, gradients, compositing and filters, with the compiler in the page: edit the paint and watch it recompile, to JavaScript or to a GPU shader.
pick an example from the menu, or edit the source; the picture updates as you type. Paint must be a texture (Point >> Color). Open The WGSL your Paint became under the pipeline to see the shader. Textures with filters paint on the CPU only.

Rendering: about 540 lines of Nile (all of gezira) and 90 of TypeScript, compiled by umgeni. The page’s interface and example textures aren’t counted.

The WGSL your Paint became 









  This Nile pipeline, PaintBeziers, takes a stream of beziers (a
  disc, a star and a thick stroke, made in Nile from shapes written in plain
  TypeScript), clips and rasterizes them, and paints them with
  Paint, the texture you write here. A texture is itself a
  pipeline, from points to colours: ApplyTexture expands the spans
  into pixels and asks Paint for each pixel's colour, and
  TypeScript writes the pixels to the canvas. The pipeline is parameterized by
  the clip rectangle and by the texture, which can itself take textures (a blur,
  a blend) as parameters. The compiler runs in this page: each edit compiles
  your Paint with gezira's gradients, compositors and filters to
  JavaScript, and, for the GPU, to a WGSL function the fragment shader calls
  for each pixel.
