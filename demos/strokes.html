Stroking with gezira's own stroke.nl

Offsets, caps, joins and the miter limit come from Dan Amelang's original stroke.nl, unmodified: it recursively subdivides curves (OffsetBezier substitutes itself mid-stream), joins each pair of segments, and strokes the path's two sides with DupCat and Reverse. Umgeni compiles all of that to plain first-order pipelines; the host only feeds cubics and composites spans. Faint white lines are the input paths.
