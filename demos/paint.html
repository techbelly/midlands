Paint

Edit a Nile texture and see it painted. The compiler runs in this page.

See the Nile pipeline 





  A texture is a pipeline from points to colours. Each edit compiles your
  Paint with gezira's library (gradients, colour spans, 25
  compositors, bilinear, bicubic and Gaussian filters) to JavaScript, and
  fills a disc, a star and a thick stroke with it. Filters take a whole
  texture as an argument; the compiler inlines it where it is used. For the
  GPU, Paint is compiled again, to a WGSL function the fragment
  shader calls for each pixel.



How to use: pick an example from the menu, or edit the
  source; the picture updates as you type. Paint must be a texture
  (Point >> Color). Open The WGSL your Paint became
  to see the shader. Textures with filters paint on the CPU only.
