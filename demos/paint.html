Paint: edit Nile, see it render

The Umgeni compiler runs in this page. Each edit compiles your text together with Dan Amelang's gezira library — gradients, colour spans, 25 compositors, bilinear, bicubic and Gaussian filters — into JavaScript, then fills a disc, a star and a thick stroke with your Paint texture. Textures are pipelines that take a point and produce a colour, so a blur or a blend takes a whole texture as its argument; the compiler inlines those arguments where they're used. On the GPU, Paint is compiled once more, into a WGSL function the fragment shader calls for every pixel: open the shader panel to see what your Nile became.
