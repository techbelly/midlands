Paint

This Nile pipeline, PaintBeziers, takes a stream of beziers (a disc, a star and a thick stroke, made in Nile from shapes written in plain TypeScript), clips and rasterizes them, and paints them with Paint, the texture you write here. A texture is itself a pipeline, from points to colours: ApplyTexture expands the spans into pixels and asks Paint for each pixel's colour, and TypeScript writes the pixels to the canvas. The pipeline is parameterized by the clip rectangle and by the texture, which can itself take textures (a blur, a blend) as parameters. The compiler runs in this page: each edit compiles your Paint with gezira's gradients, compositors and filters to JavaScript, and, for the GPU, to a WGSL function the fragment shader calls for each pixel.

This page: about 540 lines of Nile (all of gezira) and 340 lines of TypeScript (80 of them the example textures), compiled by umgeni.
